\chapter{Results}\label{results}

\section{Assessment Interviews}

After the two coders completed their assessments, the codings were gathered for \gls{irr} analysis. \autoref{fig:irr_table_ass} shows the calculated agreement percentage, \gls{irr}-coefficients and prevalence across each category. \hyperref[envnegl]{ENVNEGL} was not found in any of the transcripts by either coder, so a coefficient could not be calculated. \autoref{fig:alpha_kappa_ass} shows the level of agreement on each category. It was noticed a weak agreement (both coefficients above 0.6) in the categories of
\begin{itemize}
    \item \hyperref[cogpass]{COGPASS}
    \item \hyperref[semunder]{SEMUNDER}
    \item and \hyperref[overauto]{OVERAUTO},
\end{itemize}
a systematic disagreement (both coefficients below 0) in the categories of
\begin{itemize}
    \item \hyperref[anthro]{ANTHRO}
    \item \hyperref[realtime]{REALTIME}
    \item and \hyperref[contexneg]{CONTEXNEG},
\end{itemize}
a minor systematic disagreement (one coefficient below 0) in the categories of
\begin{itemize}
    \item \hyperref[semunder]{SEMUNDER}
    \item and \hyperref[condacc]{CONDACC},
\end{itemize}
and a minor agreement (both coefficients between 0 and 0.6) in the rest of the categories.

The final coding matrix from the assessment interviews, shown in \autoref{final_coding_ass}, was used as a baseline for the coders when coding the follow-up interviews. Misconception deemed present in the assessment interview were flipped to absent if the participants made particular statements showing awareness or understanding of the correct concept. If they did express the same opinion as they did in the assessment interview, or if they did not provide sufficient evidence to support awareness or viable understanding, the misconception was retained. \autoref{fig:combined_table_alpha_fu} shows the calculated \gls{irr} coefficients and agreement scores for the coders in the follow up interviews. From the assessment interviews, \hyperref[envnegl]{ENVNEGL}, \hyperref[plagblind]{PLAGBLIND}, and \hyperref[copyblind]{COPYBLIND} were already absent, but in the follow-up coding \hyperref[credbias]{CREDBIAS} were found absent as well. It was found perfect agreement in the categories of
\begin{itemize}
    \item CONDTREAT
    \item CONDACC
    \item and REPLFEAR,
\end{itemize}
and sufficient agreement (both coefficients above 0.6) in the categories of
\begin{itemize}
    \item OVERAUTO
    \item CONTEXNEG
    \item SOURCEATTR.
\end{itemize}
\hyperref[semunder]{SEMUNDER} was the only category with a systematic disagreement (both coefficients below 0). The rest of the categories had a minor agreement (both coefficients between 0 and 0.6). Coincidence matrices for each category are shown in \autoref{fig:coincidence_fu}.

The coders met to discuss the coding of the follow-up interviews and agreed on a final coding matrix to be used for the analysis of the results. The final coding matrix is shown in \autoref{final_coding_fu}.

\section{Shifts}
When the final coding matrix for both the assessment (\autoref{final_coding_ass}) and the follow-up interviews (\autoref{final_coding_fu}) were compared, several shifts in coding were observed. A detailed heatmap of the shifts is shown in \autoref{persistence_hm}, where a misconception is marked as resolved if it was present in the assessment but absent in the follow-up interviews.
\begin{figure}
    \centering
    \caption{Misconception Persistence Heatmap}
    \label{persistence_hm}
    \includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{figures/misconception_persistence_heatmap.png}
\end{figure}

To see any indication of a general effect of the mediation tool, we can look at the quantitative data on misconception persistence, shown in \autoref{quantitative_persistence}. Notably, all participants in the categories of
\begin{itemize}
    \item CREDBIAS
    \item SEMUNDER
    \item and HALLBLIND
\end{itemize}
were found resolved, most participants in the categories of
\begin{itemize}
    \item OVERACC
    \item and ANTHRO
\end{itemize}
were found resolved, and about half of the participants in the categories of
\begin{itemize}
    \item REALTIME
    \item DETAUTO
    \item and SOURCEATTR
\end{itemize}
were found resolved. On the other hand, no participants were found resolved in the categories of
\begin{itemize}
    \item CONDTREAT
    \item CONDACC
    \item REPLFEAR
    \item and DEPRISK.
\end{itemize}
The rest of the categories had higher persistence than resolution.
